# Example client style guide (template)

> Copy this file into your client's Notion page (the row in the Clientes
> database). The agent reads the whole page as `guia_estilo` and follows
> ONLY that client's guide. Never mix rules between clients.

## 1. Tone and voice

- Second person ("you") by default.
- Empathetic, no judgement, never alarmist.
- Direct but warm. No corporate jargon.

## 2. Length and structure

- **Usual length:** 1000-1250 words.
- Short intro hook (2-4 sentences) that validates the reader.
- H2s as real questions the reader would search for.
- Every H2 followed by a real paragraph, never a list directly.
- Short closing with a soft CTA to contact.

## 3. Lists and FAQ

- Explanatory text first, lists only when they truly help.
- Numbered lists for actionable steps, bullets for signs/examples.
- FAQ block only if it adds something new, not by routine.

## 4. Vocabulary

- Own words: list them here.
- Banned words/formulas: list them here (e.g. "Ultimate guide...", "Discover...").

## 5. Internal links and brand mentions

- 1-3 related posts with natural anchor text, never pasted URLs.
- Brand mentioned naturally mid-article or at closing, never as an interruption.
- Final contact block: phone, email, address.

## 6. Meta

- Natural meta title (max 60 chars), no clickbait.
- Natural meta description (140-156 chars), no template formulas.

## 7. Quick checklist before publishing

- [ ] H1 is the real question someone would search?
- [ ] Opening validates the reader in 2-4 sentences?
- [ ] Every H2 followed by a paragraph?
- [ ] No banned formulas?
- [ ] Links use natural anchors?
